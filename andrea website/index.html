Andrea Memba

20°C · 7:07pm in Arusha, Tanzania

I'm a photographer and filmmaker at heart, drawn to the way images can turn ordinary moments into something worth remembering.

Right now I run EchoFrame Africa, a marketing agency helping brands communicate, connect, and grow through strategy, creative, and storytelling. Working between Tanzania and Mauritius gives me two very different environments to draw from, while working with people and brands across Africa.

My work sits at the intersection of culture, people, and communication. Whether it's a campaign, a brand story, or a single frame, I care about making things feel intentional, honest, and connected to the people they're meant for.

I care about storytelling, but also about the thinking behind it: understanding the audience, finding the right idea, and knowing how to turn it into something people actually remember.
