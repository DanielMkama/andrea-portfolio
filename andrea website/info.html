I'm a photographer and filmmaker at heart, drawn to the way images can turn ordinary moments into something worth remembering.

Right now I run EchoFrame Africa, an independent visual studio where I work across photography and film, telling stories for people, brands, and places across Africa. Moving between Tanzania and Mauritius gives me two very different environments to observe, document, and create from.

My work is rooted in people, place, culture, and the details that are easy to miss. I like images that feel honest and cinematic without feeling overly constructed.

I care about storytelling, but also about the process of getting there: being present, finding the right light, waiting for the right moment, and knowing when to simply let a scene speak for itself.
